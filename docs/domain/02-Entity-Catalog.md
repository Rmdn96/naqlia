## 1. Purpose

This catalog defines every logical entity approved or reserved by Domain Model Suite v1. Every entity has one authoritative owner, lifecycle, expected operations, relationship summary, audit requirement, and deletion policy. Field and relationship details are normative in the companion catalogs.

## 2. Conventions

- **Create** means create through the owning domain's authorized command.
- **Read** includes only role-, purpose-, field-, state-, and Organization-authorized reads.
- **Update** means permitted mutable fields or governed transitions; it never means arbitrary row editing.
- **Delete** means the stated business disposition. Ordinary hard delete is forbidden unless the entity is explicitly ephemeral/derived and retention authorizes it.
- `FOUNDATION`, `MVP`, and `FUTURE` have the meanings in the [Domain Model](./01-Domain-Model.md#4-implementation-dispositions).
- Every tenant-owned entity has immutable Organization ownership even when not repeated in its description.
- “Full audit” means create, material update/transition, sensitive read/export, privileged action, and deletion/retirement evidence according to the [Audit Strategy](../05-Audit-Strategy.md).
- “Event history” means the dedicated immutable domain/history entity is the primary lifecycle evidence; application audit remains separately required for governed actions.

## 21. Entity Completeness Rule

No physical table or persistent business record may be introduced unless it maps to one entity here or an approved update adds it with owner, disposition, fields, relationships, events, audit, retention, and deletion behavior. Conversely, `FUTURE` entities MUST NOT be implemented until their product scope is approved.
