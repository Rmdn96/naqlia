# Naqlk Voice and Tone

## 1. Voice

Naqlk speaks with calm confidence. It is clear, practical, respectful, and human. It helps customers describe a real transport need without forcing them to understand logistics terminology or internal workflows.

The voice is:

- direct without being abrupt;
- reassuring without making promises the operation cannot prove;
- professional without bureaucratic language;
- Arabic-first, with natural English rather than literal translation;
- specific about the next action, required information, and expected response.

## 2. Tone by context

| Context                | Tone                         | Required behavior                                                      |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Discovery and homepage | Helpful and concise          | Explain the service and human review plainly.                          |
| Request wizard         | Guiding and low-friction     | Use short instructions, concrete examples, and visible recovery paths. |
| Confirmation           | Reassuring and precise       | Show the NQ reference and explain what happens next.                   |
| Validation or error    | Neutral and actionable       | State what needs correction without blame.                             |
| Privacy and security   | Transparent and factual      | Explain collection, purpose, access, and choices in plain language.    |
| Sales communication    | Professional and accountable | Separate customer commitments from internal notes and draft decisions. |

## 3. Arabic guidance

Arabic is the source experience. Use Modern Standard Arabic that is familiar in Saudi digital services, address the customer respectfully, and avoid unnecessary English terminology. Keep numerals and public references readable with explicit left-to-right isolation where required. The official tagline is `نقلك... ننقل كل ما يهمك`.

## 4. English guidance

English copy should sound native, compact, and service-oriented. Use `quotation`, `request`, `pickup`, and `delivery` consistently. The official English tagline is `Your move. Everything that matters.`

## 5. Prohibited patterns

- Do not claim instant pricing, guaranteed timing, live tracking, or service availability before those capabilities exist.
- Do not expose internal statuses, security details, or implementation language to customers.
- Do not use urgency, fear, excessive punctuation, or promotional superlatives.
- Do not invent contact details, social profiles, accreditations, or legal names.
- Do not use the previous brand in new customer-facing communication.
